# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Private WM and Bundesliga tipping participants use the app with family, friends, or community groups to enter match tips, bonus picks, and follow rankings. Web-only admins prepare participants, codes, schedules, results, bonus values, and release checks.

## Product Purpose

The product makes code-based participation, match tips, bonus picks, rankings, live status, and admin preparation simple and trustworthy. Success means participants understand what to do next on mobile, admins can prepare seasons without exposing unfinished data, and scoring or visibility rules are always clear.

## Brand Personality

Sportlich, klar, privat.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, over-decorated AI sports pages, confusing betting-app aesthetics, inaccessible neon-on-dark interfaces, and public or viral surfaces that weaken the private-community feeling.

## Design Principles

- Keep the matchday flow task-first: login, tip, save, compare, and return without friction.
- Prefer private trust over spectacle: visual energy is welcome only when it supports confidence.
- Make mobile the main surface: controls must be reachable, legible, and stable on narrow screens.
- Explain scoring, locks, live status, and visibility plainly at the point of use.
- Keep admin power separate from participant simplicity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, practical 40px or larger touch targets, reduced-motion alternatives, and status communication that does not rely on color alone.
